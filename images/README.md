# Image sources

`uw-madison-crest.png` is the unmodified full-color W Crest from the
[University of Wisconsin-Madison official brand resource](https://brand.wisc.edu/resource/crest-only-logo-for-web-digital-use-full-color/),
downloaded from its `uw-crest-color-web-digital.zip` bundle.

`wuhan-university-seal.png` is the standard two-color seal from
[Wuhan University's official identity page](https://www.whu.edu.cn/xxgk/wdbs.htm),
downloaded from its `xiaohui.zip` bundle without modification.

`uc-berkeley-seal.png` is the 500 px preview of the
[UC Berkeley seal on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Seal_of_University_of_California,_Berkeley.svg),
credited there to UC Berkeley and RaphaelQS and marked as public domain.
