# Blog post sources

- `sparse-mlp-neurons.md` is the English EM research note, published at `/blog/sparse-mlp-neurons/`.
- `sparse-mlp-neurons-appendix.md` is its technical appendix, published at `/blog/sparse-mlp-neurons/appendix/`.

The appendix describes the discovery samples, neuron selection, layer distribution, and evaluation datasets. Its layer-distribution figure can be rebuilt with `python scripts/build_neuron_distribution.py` (requires Matplotlib).
